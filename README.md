## 📊 Project Overview

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze the AI semiconductor industry across **market performance, company financials, chip pricing, manufacturing capacity, R&D investment, and export-control policies**.

The project follows an end-to-end analytics workflow from **data cleaning and transformation to data modeling, DAX-based KPIs, visualization, and business insights**.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

The semiconductor industry generates large volumes of market, financial, pricing, manufacturing, and policy data. Analyzing these datasets separately makes it difficult to identify trends and support business decisions.

This project integrates multiple datasets into a centralized Power BI dashboard to answer key questions related to **AI chip vendors, revenue, shipments, pricing, manufacturing, R&D, and geopolitical policies**.

---

## 📂 Dataset

The project uses four CSV datasets:

| Dataset | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `ai_chip_market.csv` | AI chip vendors, revenue, shipments, launches and product metrics |
| `chip_companies_financials.csv` | Company revenue, operating income, R&D and CAPEX |
| `chip_prices.csv` | Historical and monthly chip prices |
| `export_controls.csv` | Semiconductor export-control policies and severity |

### Dataset Coverage

- AI Chip Market: **120 records**
- Company Financials: **617 records**
- Chip Prices: **405 records**
- Export Controls: **34 records**
- Companies: **40**
- Countries: **9**
- AI Chips: **120**

## 🔑 Key Insights

- **NVIDIA** is the leading AI chip vendor by estimated revenue and shipments.
- **NVIDIA H100** is the highest revenue-generating chip in the analyzed market dataset.
- **TSMC** represents a major share of semiconductor manufacturing capacity.
- AI accelerators such as **NVIDIA H100 and B200** have significantly higher average prices than memory products.
- Major semiconductor companies maintain substantial **R&D investments** to support innovation.
- AI chip launches have increased over the analyzed period.
- **USA** accounts for the highest number of recorded export-control policies.
- Export-control severity varies significantly across years, reflecting changes in semiconductor trade policies.
